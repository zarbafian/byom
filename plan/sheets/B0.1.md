- **Verification (as landed — the commands below are the ones that run):**
  - `python3 conformance/run.py` — every schema compiles, every vector
    validates, digests re-derive, registry/bundle/descriptor structural
    checks. 462 vectors on this tree.
  - `python3 policy/eval.py check spec/vectors/policy` and
    `node policy/eval.mjs check spec/vectors/policy` — the **two independent
    BPA-1 evaluators**, which must agree byte-for-byte under JCS;
    `python3 policy/differential.py --seed 45217 --cases 256` extends the
    agreement over structured random cases.
  - `python3 family-vectors/xcheck.py` and
    `node family-vectors/tscheck/check.mjs` — the **two independent family
    rederivers** (Python stdlib / Node builtins, no shared code).
  - `python3 proof/check-descriptors.py` and `make -C proof full` —
    descriptor↔model parity and TLC on all nine models.
  - `python3 proof/negative-checks.py` — the ADR-0003 mutation suite; a
    vacuous harness fails here.
  - **Correction to the original sheet:** it named
    `python3 conformance/run.py bundles/b0.1` and
    `npm test --prefix conformance-ts`. The runner takes no bundle argument
    (the bundle is registry-derived), and no `conformance-ts` client exists.
    The "two independent clients (Python, TypeScript)" claim is **not** met
    by a second wire client: what is genuinely doubled is the policy
    evaluator and the family-vector rederiver, above. The TypeScript-side
    replay of the *operation* vectors is unbuilt.
